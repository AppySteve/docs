Your agents live wherever you already work. Connect a channel once and you can message Violet from it the same way you'd message a colleague. No separate login, no new app to learn.

All channels work the same way once connected: describe what you need in plain language, and Violet routes it to the right specialist. Results come back in the same thread where you asked.

Visit [builder.appy.ai](https://builder.appy.ai) any time to see every channel in one place. Every channel you haven't connected yet shows there with an **Add** button; every one you have shows **a check mark**.

## Slack

Appy.AI installs in your Slack workspace as an app. Once it's there, message it like any other teammate — open a direct message, or @mention it in a shared channel.

To connect, click **Add Slack** and authorize the app to access your workspace. Once connected, it's available to everyone in your organization.

## Microsoft Teams

Appy.AI works inside Microsoft Teams as a custom app. Once installed, your team can message AI agents without leaving Teams: send a DM, or @mention an agent in a channel and it responds in the thread.

Setup requires a Microsoft global administrator to upload the app and grant permissions — about 10 minutes total. Click **Add Teams** to start; you'll be offered an assisted or self-serve install path.

## Telegram

Click **Add Telegram** and Violet appears in your regular Telegram chat list, no separate app to install, no admin setup required.

## iMessage and SMS

Text Violet from your own phone number, the same way you'd text a contact, no app to install.

### Setting it up

1. Go to [builder.appy.ai/imessage/connect](https://builder.appy.ai/imessage/connect) (or click **Add iMessage** from the Dashboard).
2. **Add your number** and country code.
3. **Verify it.** You'll get a 6-digit code by text — enter it to confirm the number is yours.
4. **Text Violet first.** Once your number is verified, send her a message to start the conversation.

Once you've texted her, she's in your contacts like anyone else. If your phone can't receive iMessage, the same conversation continues over standard SMS or RCS automatically — you don't need to do anything differently.

## Email

Every agent — including Violet — has its own dedicated email address, so email works as a full channel alongside Slack, Teams, Telegram, and iMessage. Send a message to that address and the agent gets to work, using the email's subject, body, and attachments as the task.

Email doesn't require a connection step here: find Violet's address and manage who's allowed to send to it under your avatar → **Settings** → **Email**. See [Profile & Settings](/your-ai-team/administration/settings#email) for the full setup, including the sender allowlist.

## Managing your connections

Connected channels also appear on the [Dashboard](/your-ai-team/administration/dashboard), in a "Talk to Violet in..." panel, and on the [Integrations page](/your-ai-team/administration/integrations) under Communication channels. Disconnecting a channel is admin-only, and can be done from either the Dashboard or the Integrations page.
