## Create your account

Signup at [appy.ai](https://appy.ai) and, create your account using your Microsoft, Google, or email login. We'll walk you through selecting a channel to talk with Violet. You can always return to this administrative side of the platform later, or connect new channels. Supported channels:

- Slack
- Microsoft Teams
- iMessage
- Telegram
- Email

## Your first message

Open a direct message with Appy.AI in whichever channel you connected and start telling Violet what you need.

No commands to memorize, no special syntax — just describe your goal like you would to a colleague.

## Admnistrative platform

Log in to [builder.appy.ai](http://builder.appy.ai) to manage your account:

1. Monitor agent activity
2. Billing and account upgrades
3. Integrations
4. Scheduled tasks

## Inviting your team

Once Appy.AI is installed in Slack or Teams, it's available to everyone in your organization. See [Members](/your-ai-team/administration/members) 

<Tip>
  Add Appy.AI to a shared channel for team-wide use, or let team members find it in Apps to start their own direct message.
</Tip>
