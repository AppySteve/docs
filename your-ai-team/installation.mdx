Appy.AI reaches you wherever you already work, so your team gets a full AI workforce without switching to a new tool. See [Connecting Violet](/your-ai-team/connecting-violet) for every channel Appy supports, what each looks like day to day, and how to connect it.

## Create your account

When you sign up, create your account using your Microsoft or Slack login. You'll have access immediately, and can connect any channel from [builder.appy.ai/connect](https://builder.appy.ai/connect) right after.

## Your first message

Open a direct message with Appy.AI in whichever channel you connected and start telling Violet what you need.

## Web platform

Log in to [builder.appy.ai](http://builder.appy.ai) to manage your account:

1. Monitor agent activity
2. Billing and account upgrades
3. Integrations
4. Scheduled tasks

## Inviting your team

Once Appy.AI is installed in Slack or Teams, it's available to everyone in your organization. (Microsoft Teams lets you restrict access via app permissions if needed.)

<Tip>
  Add Appy.AI to a shared channel for team-wide use, or let team members find it in Apps to start their own direct message.
</Tip>
