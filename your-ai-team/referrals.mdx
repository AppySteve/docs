Every organization gets its own referral link. Share it, and both sides get credits the moment someone you invited joins — no waiting for them to subscribe to a paid plan.

## How it works

1. **Get your link.** Open your avatar (bottom-left of the navigation) and choose **Refer & Earn**.
2. **Share it.** Copy your personal invite link, or send it directly to up to 20 email addresses at once from the same dialog.
3. **They sign up.** As soon as the person you invited creates an organization through your link, the referral counts — they don't need to pick a paid plan first.
4. **You both get credits.** You and the organization you referred each receive **40,000 reward credits**, automatically, with no action needed on either side.

Reward credits are separate from your plan's monthly allowance — they never expire and show up on your [Dashboard](/your-ai-team/administration/dashboard#reward-credits) and [Billing](/your-ai-team/administration/billing) pages as their own line.

## Tracking your referrals

The **Rewards** tab in the same Refer & Earn dialog shows:

- **Total credits earned** from referrals so far
- **Every organization you've referred**, with the credits each one earned you

## The lifetime cap

An organization can earn up to **400,000 lifetime credits** from referring others. If you're close to the cap, referrals you make still work normally for the organization joining — they still get their 40,000 credits — you just stop earning more yourself once you've hit the limit.

<Note>
  The cap applies only to what you can earn as a referrer. The organization you refer always gets their full signup bonus, regardless of where you are against your own cap.
</Note>
