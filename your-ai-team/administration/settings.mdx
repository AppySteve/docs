Your profile and account settings live in a modal accessible from your avatar at the bottom-left of the navigation. There are three tabs: **Profile**, **Organization**, and **Email**.

## Profile

The Profile tab is where you manage your personal account information.

<Frame>
  ![Settings modal open to the Profile tab](/images/settings-profile-tab.png)
</Frame>

**What you can update:**

- **Profile photo** — Click the photo to upload a new image.
- **First and last name** — Edit directly in the fields and save.
- **Email addresses** — Your primary email is shown with a `Primary` badge. Add additional addresses using the input field and **Add** button.
- **Password** — Click **Set password** to create or update your login password.
- **Connected accounts** — Link third-party sign-in providers (e.g., Google) for one-click login. If none are connected, this section reads "No connected accounts."

## Organization

The Organization tab shows your organization's name, logo, and member count, along with your public URL slug. Name and logo are admin-editable; the slug is admin-editable too.

Looking to invite people or manage roles? That lives on its own page now — see [Members](/your-ai-team/administration/members).

If you need to leave the organization entirely, the **Leave Organization** button is available at the bottom of this tab.

## Email

The Email tab controls who can reach Violet by email, and is visible only to organization admins.

**Violet's Email Address** — every organization has a dedicated inbound address for Violet, shown here with a copy-to-clipboard button. Send a message to this address and Violet processes it as a task, the same way she would a Slack DM.

**Access control is an allowlist, not a blocklist.** Only mail from a sender on this list reaches Violet — everything else is rejected outright, not queued for review. If someone emails Violet and nothing happens, this is the first place to check.

- **Allowed Domains** — any email from these domains is accepted (e.g., `company.com`). Add a domain and it covers every address at that domain.
- **Allowed Email Addresses** — specific addresses that can email Violet, independent of domain.
- **Blocked Email Addresses** — specific addresses to reject even when their domain is otherwise allowed. A block always overrides an allow, so you can permit a whole company domain and still exclude one address within it.

<Note>
  Only organization admins can view or change the Email tab's settings.
</Note>
