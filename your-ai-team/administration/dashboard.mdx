The Dashboard is your home on the Appy.AI web platform. It gives you an at-a-glance read on your account status and surfaces the fastest paths to getting your agents to work.

## At a glance

Three summary tiles sit at the top of the Dashboard:

- **Credits** — Your combined credit balance: your plan's monthly allowance plus any [reward credits](#reward-credits) you've earned, shown as a single total. Credits refresh on your billing cycle. On complimentary accounts, this tile reads "Unlimited."
- **Schedules** — The number of active scheduled tasks your agents are running automatically.
- **Integrations** — How many third-party tools your agents are connected to.

### Reward credits

Reward credits are bonus credits earned separately from your plan's monthly allowance — for example, through referrals. They're folded into the total shown on this tile, but if you want to see the reward balance on its own, check the [Billing](/your-ai-team/administration/billing) page, where it's broken out as its own line.

## Connect your communication channels

If you haven't set up Slack or Microsoft Teams yet, the Dashboard walks you through it. Your agents live in chat — connecting a channel is what lets you talk to them.

- **Connect Slack** — Install the Appy.AI app in your workspace to message agents directly in channels and DMs.
- **Connect Teams** — Add Appy.AI to Microsoft Teams to run agents from any chat or channel.

See [Installation](/your-ai-team/installation) for step-by-step setup.

## Quick links

At the bottom of the Dashboard:

- **Documentation** — Opens this help center in a new tab.
- **Book office hours** — Schedule up to 30 minutes with the Appy.AI team for hands-on help.
- **Contact Support** — Report a bug or share feedback directly with the team.
