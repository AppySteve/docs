The Dashboard is your home on the Appy.AI web platform. It gives you an at-a-glance read on your account status and surfaces the fastest paths to getting your agents to work.

## At a glance

Three summary tiles sit at the top of the Dashboard:

- **Credits** — Your combined credit balance: your plan's monthly allowance plus any [reward credits](#reward-credits) you've earned, shown as a single total. Credits refresh on your billing cycle.
- **Schedules** — The number of active scheduled tasks your agents are running automatically.
- **Integrations** — How many third-party tools your agents are connected to.

### Reward credits

Reward credits are bonus credits earned separately from your plan's monthly allowance — for example, through referrals. They're folded into the total shown on this tile, but if you want to see the reward balance on its own, check the [Billing](/your-ai-team/administration/billing) page, where it's broken out as its own line.

## Connect your communication channels

Your agents live in chat and email — connecting a channel is what lets you talk to them. The Dashboard shows a "Talk to Violet in..." panel listing all four channels:

- **Slack** — Message agents directly in channels and DMs.
- **Teams** — Run agents from any chat or channel in Microsoft Teams.
- **Telegram** — Chat with Violet from your regular Telegram chat list.
- **Email** — Task your agents by sending a message to Violet's dedicated address.

A connected channel shows a checkmark; an unconnected one shows an **Add** button. Click **Add** to connect that channel on the spot.

See [Installation](/your-ai-team/installation) for step-by-step setup on each channel.

### The Connect page

Every channel connection also lives on one dedicated page: visit [builder.appy.ai/connect](https://builder.appy.ai/connect) to see and connect Slack, Teams, Telegram, and Email in one place. It's the same four channels as the Dashboard's panel — useful if you want a single screen to finish connecting every channel at once, or to add another platform later.
