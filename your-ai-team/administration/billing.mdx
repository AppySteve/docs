The Billing page is where you manage your Appy.AI subscription, payment method, and credit usage.

## Which billing model applies to you

Not every account sees the same Billing page. Appy has three billing states, and which one you're on determines what this page shows:

- **Credits model** — Your workspace has a monthly credit allowance based on your plan, consumed as your agents work. This is the current default for new accounts. See [Pricing](/pricing) for plan and credit details.
- **Legacy model** — Some earlier accounts are on a subscription structure that predates the credits model, with its own free-execution allowance and subscription states.
- **Complimentary access** — A small number of organizations have complimentary access and aren't billed at all.

If the sections below don't match what you see, it's because your account is on a different one of these three — that's expected, not a bug.

## Credits model: usage and plan management

If you're on the credits model, the Billing page shows:

- **Your plan and credit balance** — Current tier, credits remaining, and a **Reward credits** line for any bonus credits you've earned separately from your plan allowance.
- **Execution credit usage table** — A per-execution breakdown of credit consumption: who initiated it, what the activity was, which channel it came through, and how many credits it used. Filter by time period (last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or all time) and search across entries.

### Switching plans in place

Upgrading or downgrading doesn't send you back through checkout. Pick a new plan and confirm — an upgrade takes effect immediately with a prorated charge to your card on file and a refreshed credit allotment; a downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep your current credit cap until then with no charge today.

## Selecting a plan

If you haven't subscribed yet, choose your monthly cost and credit allocation and proceed through a secure Stripe checkout to purchase.

## Managing your subscription

Once your payment method is on file, the Billing page lets you upgrade, manage your subscription, and cancel.

## Canceling your subscription

Click **Manage Subscription** and follow the cancellation steps. Your access continues through the end of the current billing period. There are no cancellation fees.

## Past-due payments

If a payment fails, you'll see a banner here: **"Payment failed — your subscription is past due."** Update your payment method to restore access — agent interactions pause until it's resolved.
